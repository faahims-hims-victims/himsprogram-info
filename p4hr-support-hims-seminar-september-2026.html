P4HR's HIMS Seminar advocacy campaign: thank you for your support—and send us the issues you want raised.

P4HR News • September 9, 2026

Thank You for Standing With P4HR. Today, We Take Your Voice to the HIMS Seminar.

P4HR is moving forward because pilots, families, professionals, advocates, and donors have chosen to stand behind this movement. As the HIMS Seminar begins today, we want you to know exactly what that support is helping make possible—and how you can help shape the conversations we have while we are there.

First and foremost: thank you

We could not be where we are today without you.

Support for Pilots for HIMS Reform has come in many forms. Some of you have shared experiences that were difficult to put into words. Some have provided records, research, expertise, introductions, or professional insight. Some have amplified our work publicly. Others have supported us quietly because speaking openly about HIMS can still carry real professional concerns.

And many of you have supported this movement financially.

Recently, P4HR has been especially encouraged by several meaningful financial contributions, along with the continued support of donors giving at every level. Those contributions are giving this organization greater capacity to travel, attend key industry events, expand research and policy work, communicate directly with decision-makers, and build public awareness around the need for HIMS reform.

Every form of support matters. A financial contribution can help us put resources behind the work. A personal story can identify a policy problem. A document can expose a contradiction. A conversation can open a door. A share can put the issue in front of someone who has never heard it before.

The HIMS Seminar begins today—and P4HR is there

Today, September 9, the HIMS Seminar begins. P4HR will be there throughout the event representing the reform perspective and, most importantly, representing the pilots and families whose experiences too often go unheard in rooms where HIMS policy and practice are discussed.

We are not attending simply to observe.

We intend to listen carefully, ask difficult but fair questions, challenge assumptions when the evidence requires it, and advocate for a system that is transparent, evidence-based, objectively administered, safety-focused, and capable of recognizing long-term recovery rather than treating historical diagnoses as permanent regulatory identities.

Where useful and appropriate, we are also prepared to seek direct conversations with FAA personnel, HIMS stakeholders, aeromedical professionals, policymakers, and others who can help address specific concerns.

What do you want us to raise while we are there?
If there is a specific HIMS policy, practice, experience, question, inconsistency, or reform priority you believe needs to be put in front of the people attending this seminar, send it to us. We want to hear it.

Send us the issue you want advocated

If you have something specific you want P4HR to advocate for on your behalf during the seminar, please contact us as soon as possible. Tell us what happened, what policy or practice concerns you, and—if you know—what you would like changed.

That may include concerns involving monitoring duration, testing requirements, Special Issuance, step-down decisions, psychiatric evaluations, treatment requirements, HIMS AME authority, transparency, appeals, unrestricted medical certification, privacy, financial burdens, or another issue that you believe deserves attention.

We cannot promise that every individual issue will result in a specific meeting or immediate answer. But where a concern is appropriate to raise, we can use the opportunity to seek answers, identify the right decision-maker, advocate for the broader policy issue, or request a follow-up conversation after the seminar.

We will not identify you or disclose personal details without your permission. If you want us to raise an issue only in generalized or de-identified form, tell us that when you write.

Send P4HR an Issue to Raise

The next phase of P4HR

This movement is entering a more active phase.

Our work will continue on multiple fronts: the FAA regulatory process, legislative advocacy, engagement with oversight bodies, direct policy conversations, public education, media outreach, research, and strategic communications. The objective is not simply to criticize HIMS. It is to build the evidence, public understanding, policy alternatives, and sustained pressure necessary to achieve meaningful reform.

We intend to pursue that work aggressively—but also professionally, factually, and strategically. Reform will require persistence. It will require being present in the rooms where the system is discussed, and it will require creating new rooms when affected pilots have been excluded from the conversation.

Your support is what allows us to keep doing that.

Your support is becoming action

When you support P4HR, you are helping move this effort beyond online discussion and into direct advocacy. You are helping us attend events like the HIMS Seminar, pursue regulatory and legislative avenues, examine the evidence, communicate with decision-makers, and make sure the pilot perspective is no longer missing from discussions about pilot medical policy.

For those who have donated, shared your story, offered your expertise, introduced us to someone who can help, or simply encouraged us to keep going: thank you.

We are moving forward. And we intend to make the most of every opportunity in front of us.

Help us keep showing up.

Financial support helps P4HR sustain research, outreach, travel, policy engagement, and public education as the reform campaign expands.

About P4HR: Pilots for HIMS Reform is an independent pilot advocacy organization seeking transparent, evidence-based, time-limited, and safety-focused reform of FAA HIMS medical certification and monitoring practices. P4HR is not affiliated with the FAA or the official HIMS Program.
